## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces** (soft serif, expressive — never Inter/Roboto/Arial)
- Body: **Source Sans 3**
- Kickers: 11–14 px, uppercase, tracking 0.22em, yellow or pod
- Concept titles: 64–96 px, cream, leading 0.95
- One supporting sentence max per beat (≤ 14 words)

## Motion grammar (Anthropic Academy feel)

1. **Stillness first** — hold the idea before animating.
2. **One idea per beat** — never stack stats, chips, or cards.
3. **Soft fades + 24–32 px rise** — GSAP 0.7–1.0s, ease `power2.out`.
4. **No glow, no purple gradients, no emoji storms, no floating badges.**
5. **Atmosphere** — radial warm light or soft grain; not flat single color alone.
6. **End with a verb** — Enter / Start / Preorder — one CTA word.

## Aspect & length

- Landscape 1920×1080 for campus hubs and Masters
- Target intros: **18–28 s** (micro ≤ 22 s; MOOC/campus ≤ 28 s)
- FPS: 30

## Pedagogy beats (every intro)

1. Brand / track ownership (1.5–2.5 s)
2. Concept in one line
3. Why it matters (learner outcome)
4. Mental model (2–3 labeled steps max)
5. CTA into the interactive lesson
